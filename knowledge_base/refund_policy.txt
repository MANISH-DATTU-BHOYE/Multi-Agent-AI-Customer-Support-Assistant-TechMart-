TechMart Electronics - Refund Policy

Refunds are accepted within 30 days of purchase for unused, unopened products in original packaging.
To request a refund, provide your order ID and reason for return through the chat assistant or email.
Refunds are processed within 5-7 business days after the returned item passes quality inspection.
Subscription plan refunds: Premium subscription refunds are only available within 7 days of the
billing date, and only if the Premium features were not used.
Digital products and downloadable software are non-refundable once activated.

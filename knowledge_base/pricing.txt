TechMart Electronics - Pricing & Plans

Basic Plan: Free - access to standard product catalog and community support.
Premium Plan: $9.99/month - priority support, extended warranty, exclusive discounts.
Premium is billed monthly and can be cancelled anytime from Account Settings.
If you paid for Premium but features remain locked, this is usually resolved by logging out
and logging back in; if the issue persists it is escalated to the technical team.
